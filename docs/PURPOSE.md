# Purpose: Docenda

> **Protected.** Changes only with Victor's approval, along with a summary of the impact.

- **Problem:** putting together a Computing lesson plan aligned with the BNCC and adapted to the real class
  (difficulties, prior knowledge, social context, accessibility, point in the semester)
  takes time. Student teachers on internship also need to link Computing to other subjects.
  Ready-made templates are generic and ordinary AIs invent skills and sources.
- **Audience:** basic-education Computing teachers and student teachers on internship,
  including those who teach other subjects and want to link them to Computing.
- **The product must guarantee:**
  1. The plan follows the chosen template (internship script or ENADE) and the AI only adapts the content to the context.
  2. Skills only from the official BNCC Computing catalog, and sources only real ones, verified
     and approved by the teacher before they enter the plan.
  3. The author decides: the AI suggests, the author accepts, edits or rejects, and the system makes clear
     what came from the AI. Each step (script, plan, slides, standardized script) only starts
     after the author approves the previous one. The reviewers invited by the student teacher
     (the university advisor and the school supervisor) evaluate the plan; script
     and slides only reach them if the student teacher chooses to send them.
  4. No identifiable student: the class is described in aggregate, with no name,
     diagnosis or medical report.
  5. Only authenticated users use the AI, with a per-person usage limit and a spending cap.
- **The product does not:**
  - Replace the teacher or approve plans on its own.
  - Evaluate students, store individual student data or suggest diagnoses.
  - Act as a teaching platform: it does not distribute lessons or activities to students.
  - Use anyone's plans or evaluations to improve the system without explicit,
    separate consent.
  - For now, cover skills from other BNCC components; the link to other
    subjects is described in text.
- **How we know it worked:**
  - The internship's supervising teacher, using the same criterion as for the 3 reference plans,
    considers a generated plan usable with few edits.
  - A teacher creates a lesson adapted to the class in under 15 minutes.
  - No invented skill or source makes it into a saved plan.
  - A review of the stored data finds no information that identifies a student.
